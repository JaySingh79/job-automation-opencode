### Observation 1: Parallel Sector-Based Subagent Decomposition for Multi-Constraint Startup Research

**Status:** OPEN
**Date:** 2026-10-17
**Session context:** Extensive web search to find startups/companies hiring in India or globally remote that raised funding in the last 12 months (Oct 2025 – Oct 2026) and have >₹400 Crores (~$48M+ USD) in annual revenue/ARR.
**Skill:** New skill candidate: startup-market-intelligence
**Type:** open-source
**Phase/Area:** Research & Discovery Decomposition

**Issue:** Single-query web searches for multi-constraint company discovery (e.g., funding recency + revenue floor + geographic/remote hiring policy) yield generic listicles or incomplete intersections because financial metrics (MCA/RoC filings in INR crores vs. SaaS ARR in USD) and funding announcements are indexed separately from engineering career footprints.

**Suggested improvement:** Create a structured research workflow/skill that decomposes multi-constraint market mapping into parallel tracks: (1) Domestic/Regional Startup Track querying local financial trackers (e.g., Entrackr/Fintrackr, Inc42, Tracxn) by sector clusters, and (2) Global Remote/GCC Scaleup Track querying global ARR trackers (e.g., Sacra, Latka, Dealroom) paired with local entity/careers verification.

**Principle:** When filtering companies across orthogonal constraints (recent funding, revenue floor, and hiring geography), decompose queries into sector-batched entity lists and cross-verify financial filings against recent funding rounds in parallel rather than relying on a single combined keyword query.
